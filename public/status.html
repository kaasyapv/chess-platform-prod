Can't load your class?

If the page is blank or won't load, the platform may be temporarily down. Every scheduled class has a backup meeting link that only your academy's manager can see — they can send it to you directly.

Step 1. Message your coach or academy manager on WhatsApp (or however you normally reach them) and say the platform isn't loading.
Step 2. They'll send you a Google Meet or Zoom link for this class — join that instead.
Step 3. Once the platform is back, resume there as normal.
